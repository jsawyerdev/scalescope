# Code of Conduct

ScaleScope follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions) and
when you represent the project elsewhere.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and critique
ideas rather than people. Harassment, insults, and personal attacks are not
accepted.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at
james@jamessawyer.co.uk. Reports are handled privately, and the reporter's
identity is not shared without their permission. The maintainer applies the
Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
